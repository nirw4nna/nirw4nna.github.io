Hello, internet world!

Welcome to my blog. Here I'll write about stuff I'm currently working on, mostly programming-related. More specifically, at least for the foreseeable future, things related to the development of [DSC](https://github.com/nirw4nna/dsc) a GPU-accelerated tensor library I wrote from scratch.

To make things more interesting I set a [fun little challange for me](https://x.com/nirw4nna/status/1937513290789454108): to make DSC comparable, in terms of inference speed, with PyTorch on a dense LLM by the end of 2025. Will see how this goes, in any case there will be a lot to learn on this journey so, if you find this kind of things interesting, I encourage you to follow along with me!

I plan to post longer-form content here roughly once a month where I go in-depth in some topics and shorter updates daily/weekly on Twitter/X.

By the way, DSC is completely open-source so if you like this kind of programming you can even join me for the ride :)
